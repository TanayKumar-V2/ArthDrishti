## Priority issues

- **[P1] Choice overload:** Customer, officer, and admin surfaces expose many destinations. Organize around role-specific jobs and progressively reveal secondary analysis. Suggested: `$impeccable distill`.
- **[P1] Analytical density:** Dense cards, tiny labels, and badges make the primary decision harder to find. Lead with the decision, consequence, evidence, and next action. Suggested: `$impeccable layout`.
- **[P1] Financial-claim provenance:** Scores, forecasts, and savings claims need visible source, date, confidence, and limitations. Do not invent these values; connect them to supplied product data. Suggested: `$impeccable clarify`.
- **[P2] Microtype and jargon:** Reserve very small text for tertiary metadata and explain domain terms at decision points. Suggested: `$impeccable typeset`.
- **[P2] Recovery and reassurance:** Make progress, cancel/back, save state, and recovery guidance consistent in onboarding, uploads, decisions, and settings. Suggested: `$impeccable harden`.

## Cognitive load and emotional journey

The confident analytics shell can turn into uncertainty when jargon or model outputs lack context. High-stakes scores need an as-of date, source, confidence/limits, and next action. Some illustrative-looking values need clear labeling as examples.
